Privacy Policy — Noor App

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Your privacy is important to us in Noor (com.nooros.quran.app). This policy explains how data is handled:

  • Local Offline Data: Your Quran reading and memorization progress, Adhkar counters, favorites, and downloaded audio recitations are stored locally on your device only.
  • Location Data (GPS): Location permission is used solely when requested by the user to calculate accurate Prayer Times and Qibla direction. We do not store or sell your location data.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): The app integrates Google AdMob SDK (Publisher ID: pub-9202014319573083), which may collect device advertising identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Contact: For privacy inquiries, please contact us via the developer email listed on our Google Play Store page.
